Zack Personal Mail

A private email-management utility operated by Zack Exley for his own email accounts. It is not a public service and has no public registration.

The utility uses the Gmail API to review mail, identify bulk and automated messages, apply labels, and support owner-directed contact review and mailing-list unsubscribe workflows. Processing runs on private computers and servers authorized by the account owner.

This website describes the utility. It does not display email contents, contact records, credentials, or processing results.
