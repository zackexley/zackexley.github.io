Zack Personal Mail

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026.

Zack Personal Mail is operated by Zack Exley for his own accounts. Access to Google data is granted through Google's authorization flow.

Data and purposes

The utility accesses email headers, participants, message and thread identifiers, dates, subjects, message text, labels, and relevant mailing-list headers. It uses this information to organize the owner's mail, identify bulk or automated messages, apply labels, review contacts and relationships, and carry out unsubscribe instructions. Gmail settings permissions may also be granted for owner-directed mail-management functions.

Storage and access

OAuth credentials and private processing records are stored on the owner's authorized computers and servers. Processing records may include excerpts, contact details, classifications, checkpoints, and action logs. These records support resumable processing and review and are retained until the owner deletes them. Email contents and contact records are not published on this website.

The owner and assistants authorized by the owner may review the data to perform these functions. Analysis may use local models or, when directed by the owner, external AI services. Only data relevant to the authorized workflow should be provided to those services. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this utility to train general-purpose AI models. Google user data is used and transferred in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access through Google Account permissions and can delete locally stored credentials and processing records. Revocation stops future API access but does not automatically delete existing local records or undo labels already applied.

For access, deletion, or privacy questions, contact the operator at zackexley@gmail.com.
